FTP is the abreviation for File Transfer Protocol. As the name suggests, it is simply about transferring files from one computer to another.
FTP has been around since 1971!!!

In the early days, the entire internet was unencrypted; anyone could read whatever was out there. This attracted criminals who exploited the situation.

That is why the original FTP is rarely used nowadays; instead, the encrypted variant, SFTP, is used.
